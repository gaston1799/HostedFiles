SlinkerAIO Privacy Policy

Last updated: October 12, 2025

1. Data We Access

SlinkerAIO processes Discord usernames, server identifiers, channel metadata, and message content required to execute commands such as setup, verification tickets, and notifications. We do not collect or store personal data outside of Discord unless explicitly noted.

2. Temporary Storage

Captcha tickets and configuration settings are cached so the bot can complete verification and automation tasks. Ticket data is automatically cleared once the verification flow finishes or the channel is deleted.

3. Third-Party Services

When enabled, SlinkerAIO may request public data from YouTube or Twitch APIs to check for new uploads or live streams. These requests are limited to the channels you configure.

4. Data Retention

Basic server settings persist across restarts so the bot can restore your configuration. We do not maintain long-term logs of messages or member data beyond what is required to operate.

5. Your Choices

Remove the bot from your server to stop all data processing. You can also request deletion of stored configurations by contacting the maintainers.

6. Contact

For privacy questions, open a support ticket in the project repository or use the contact details provided there.
